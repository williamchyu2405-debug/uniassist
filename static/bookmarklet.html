⚠ Bookmarklet blocked by your portal? Use the Console Snippet instead

Some university portals block bookmarklets via Content-Security-Policy. The console snippet runs the same capture logic but bypasses that restriction — paste it directly into your browser's DevTools console.

  1. Navigate to the slide / module page you want to capture.
  2. Open DevTools: press F12 (Windows/Linux) or Cmd+Opt+J (Mac).
  3. Click the Console tab.
  4. Click inside the console, paste the snippet below, then press Enter.
  5. Type a subject when the prompt appears — done!

The snippet talks directly to your local UniAssist — nothing is sent anywhere else. If UniAssist moves to a new address, it asks you to update the URL automatically.
